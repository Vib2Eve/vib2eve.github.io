Research overview

Two sides of a conversation.
One motion-sensor stream.

Vib²Eve studies conversation privacy in VR headsets. Speech from the wearer and audio played by the headset loudspeaker can produce different vibration patterns in the accelerometer stream. The research examines how these sources can be distinguished and used to recover complementary conversational cues.

The two branches provide different forms of evidence: keywords and digits from the wearer, and reconstructed speech from the loudspeaker. Painting U examines these fragments together, using source attribution and context to support tentative privacy-profile inference.

CENTRAL QUESTION

What can fragmented evidence reveal about a conversation—and about the person wearing the headset?

This website presents the research structure and a text-evidence demonstration. The signal-processing stages are shown as a conceptual workflow.

02 / METHOD

From vibration to attributed evidence

The research workflow separates the two vibration sources before combining their recovered evidence.

01

Distinguish the sources

Separate wearer-induced and loudspeaker-induced vibration windows using their spectral shape and vibration intensity.

02

Recover complementary cues

Retain wearer keywords and digits alongside reconstructed remote speech. Group remote segments by anonymous speaker.

03

Reason with attribution

Combine time-aligned fragments while preserving their sources. Leave unsupported attributes undetermined.

04 / PROMPT DESIGN

The Painting U prompt

Painting U instantiates its prompt according to the privacy attributes considered in a scenario. A shared attribution policy is combined with guidance for each target dimension.

↳

Attribute first, then combine.

Make reliable connections between pieces of evidence where appropriate, but do not speculate. Fragments without a clear source remain unassigned.

01

Identity

Prefer explicit cues about the wearer. Require corroboration when identity evidence is incomplete.

02

Scene

Use the interaction context to infer the underlying scenario, beyond isolated keywords.

03

Age group

Use explicit age cues when available; otherwise limit inference to a supported, coarse range.

04

Financial sensitivity

Interpret financial evidence together with the magnitude and context of numerical amounts.

Painting U: Reconstruction and Privacy Profile Inference

[Task]
1. Reconstruct a plausible dialogue from both streams.
   Complete only information supported by the recovered evidence.
2. Infer the wearer's profile along four attributes:
   identity, scene, age group, and financial sensitivity.

[Reasoning Rules]
- Evidence attribution:
  Attribute each cue to its source before drawing conclusions.
  Make reliable connections between pieces of evidence where appropriate,
  but do not speculate.
  Unattributable fragments remain unassigned.

- Identity:
  Prefer explicit identity cues. When such cues are incomplete,
  infer identity only from corroborating evidence concerning the wearer;
  otherwise return "unknown".

- Scene:
  Infer the underlying interaction or business scenario from contextual
  evidence rather than individual keywords.

- Age group:
  When no explicit age cue is available, infer only a coarse age range
  from supported role and contextual evidence.

- Financial sensitivity:
  Consider both the financial evidence itself and the contextual
  significance of numerical amounts.

[Output Constraint]
Return one valid JSON object with no additional text:
{
  "dialogue": "...",
  "identity":
    {"label": "", "confidence": 0.0, "evidence": []},
  "scene":
    {"label": "", "confidence": 0.0, "evidence": []},
  "age_group":
    {"label": "", "confidence": 0.0, "evidence": []},
  "financial_sensitivity":
    {"label": "", "confidence": 0.0, "evidence": []}
}
